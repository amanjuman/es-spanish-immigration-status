Privacy

This is a small, self-hosted, unofficial tool. Here is exactly what it stores and why:

  • For one-off checks: the expediente number, presentation date and birth year you enter are used to query the government portal and are kept only briefly in memory (a short result cache, ~15 minutes); nothing is written to the database.
  • For monitors: the same three fields plus an optional label are stored so the background checker can re-check them. The last known status and a short check history are kept alongside.
  • For alerts: the Telegram chat id of each subscriber (never a phone number) is stored so alerts can be delivered.

Who can see your data: only whoever holds your monitor's secret management link, subscribers you invite via activation links, and the instance operator (who has server access). Other visitors cannot list or view monitors{% if public_mode %} on this instance{% endif %}.

Deletion: delete your monitor at any time from its management page — this removes the expediente data, its history and all subscriptions immediately. Monitors are also purged automatically a while after a final resolution{% if public_mode %}, and unactivated monitors are removed after a few days{% endif %}. Check history is kept at most 90 days.

Third parties: status data comes from the official portal (infoext2.delegaciondelgobierno.gob.es). Alerts go through the Telegram Bot API.{% if turnstile_site_key %} Form submissions are verified by Cloudflare Turnstile.{% endif %} No analytics, no ads, no data sales.
